This page is still under construction — partly because the website is new, and partly because research takes longer than `git push`.

I am currently working on problems in affective computing, large language models, and spatio-temporal forecasting. Several projects are at different stages of development, from experiments and manuscripts to submissions.

My interests are relatively broad, but I care most about problems where a clear research question can lead to a technically meaningful result.

Papers, preprints, and other research outputs will be listed here once they are publicly available.
